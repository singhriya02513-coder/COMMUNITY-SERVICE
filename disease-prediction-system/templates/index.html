    Good to know

    Frequently asked questions

    Everything you should know before using the symptom checker.

    Privacy first

    No sign-up, no database and no cookies for tracking. Predictions happen in memory and are never saved.

    How accurate is the prediction?

    The deployed model scores {{ metrics.accuracy }}% on held-out test data and {{ metrics.cv_accuracy }}% in 5-fold cross-validation. However, this is an educational dataset, so real-world accuracy will be lower. Treat the result as a learning aid, not medical advice.

    How many symptoms should I select?

    Choose at least {{ min_symptoms }} and up to {{ max_symptoms }} symptoms. More relevant symptoms usually produce a clearer prediction. Only select symptoms you have actually experienced.

    Why are three conditions shown?

    The classifier calculates a probability for every condition it knows. The website displays the three highest probabilities so you can see when several conditions are close. A lower confidence score means the symptoms are less distinctive.

    Is my health data stored?

    No. Your symptom selections are sent to the local server only for the prediction request. The app does not use a database, user accounts or analytics, and nothing is written to disk.

    Can this replace a doctor?

    No, never. The tool cannot examine you, order tests or understand your medical history. If your symptoms are severe, persistent or worrying, consult a qualified healthcare professional.
